Mirae Design Trading Inc. is a company that takes environmental issues seriously. The company has partnered with E-Plan Co., Ltd., a Japanese company that developed a high-quality alkaline ionized water manufacturing machine called "e-WASH." Through this partnership, Mirae Design Trading Inc. conducts sales, promotional activities, and environmental protection projects in the Philippines to support cleaner and more sustainable solutions.

The company offers the e-WASH product, a multifaceted solution for sanitation and hygiene that leverages cutting-edge technology to ensure both efficiency and safety. Our solution is designed to address the growing demand for sustainable sanitation management practices, particularly in hotels, restaurants, hospitals, schools, factories, farms, public facilities, and more, with a positive impact on the environment.
