e-WASH Points

High Safety Low Risk

e-WASH boasts high safety as its ingredients consist only of pure water and trace amounts of potassium ions. It eliminates concerns about chemical burns, skin irritation, and accidents due to misuse. Furthermore, its powerful rust-preventive properties protect iron from rust.

Excellent Cleaning Power Cleaning

With e-WASH, you can easily break down and clean away stubborn grease and grime in bathrooms and kitchens, as well as on exhaust fans, thanks to its powerful cleaning action.

High Disinfecting Effect Sanitize

Most bacteria, viruses, and other microorganisms cannot survive when they come into contact with e-WASH, which has a pH of 12.5. Thanks to e-WASH's powerful cleaning and penetrating properties, it reaches even the germs lurking within the dirt.

Anaerobic odor Prevention Effect Deodorant

Its strong alkaline properties suppress oxidation and decay, which are the source of unpleasant odors. It also reduces unpleasant odors from cigarettes, ammonia, and drains.

High Rust Prevention Effect Rust-Proofing / Anti-Oxidation

The cause of rust in iron products is oxidation. e-WASH, with a pH above 12.5, suppresses oxidation and therefore will not cause iron products to rust.

Improved Work Efficiency Saves Time Productivity

Because it doesn't produce foam, rinsing and wiping are easy, significantly reducing work time. As it's a sodium-free, highly alkaline electrolyzed water, it protects iron from rust. Its high cleaning power can be utilized regardless of experience or skill level.

Cost Reduction Cost Reduction

Approximately 90% of everyday dirt can be removed with e-WASH. Reduce the amount of detergent you use, saving money on household expenses and costs. Fewer rinses and increased work efficiency also reduce unnecessary energy costs. Furthermore, if stored in a sealed container, its functionality and performance will be maintained for a long time. It does not deteriorate due to temperature or UV light, which helps reduce costs.

Environmental Protection Eco-Friendly

e-WASH is made up of only 99.83% pure water and 0.17% potassium hydroxide. It is colorless, odorless, and removes 90% of dirt without creating foam. The wastewater turns into soap components and does not pollute the sea or rivers, making it environmentally friendly.
