KITCHEN

Washing ingredients keeps them fresh and safe, and it's also great for cleaning and disinfecting cooking utensils!

You'll really appreciate the ease of use of e-WASH when it comes to maintaining hygiene in your kitchen.

STOVE

01

Heat up the e-WASH

Pour the undiluted e-WASH, or a solution diluted to 2:1 volume and boiled, into a basin.

02

Soak the parts

Soak the burner cap and other parts for 5-10 minutes.

03

Scrub & Rinse

Scrub with a sponge and rinse with tap water.

CUTTING BOARD

01

Spray it on

Spray e-WASH evenly into the small scratches on the cutting board.

02

Scrub & Rinse

Scrub away dirt with a sponge and rinse with tap water.

MICROWAVE

01

Spray & Wipe

For regular cleaning, simply spray the cleaner directly into the oven and wipe it off.

02

Deep Clean

Add 30ml of e-WASH and heat until it evaporates inside.

LIVING ROOM

Using e-WASH to clean furniture and other items makes removing dirt easy.

Furthermore, its antibacterial and deodorizing properties allow you to safely address unpleasant odors.

TABLE

01

Spray it on

Spray e-WASH evenly onto the soiled area.

02

Wipe

Wipe with a dry cloth after waiting a few seconds.

FLOOR

01

Dilute

Dilute it to more than 10 times its original concentration.

02

Spray & Wipe

Spray diluted e-WASH and wipe away the loosened dirt.

ACCESSORIES & CLOTHES

Keep your personal items and clothes fresh and clean every day.

e-WASH effectively removes oils and odors from common accessories.
