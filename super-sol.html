Super Sol is an artificial porous lightweight foam made by crushing and firing waste glass. viewing it not as trash but as a new resource. Super Sol is a high-value, artificial porous lightweight foaming material (pumice) made by crushing, baking, and foaming waste glass. It is an environmentally friendly, soil-restoration material with excellent water permeability, retention, and fireproof properties.
