e-WASH is a disinfectant developed by E-PLAN Co., Ltd., composed of 99.83% pure water and 0.17% potassium hydroxide, resulting in a pH level of 12.5. This alkaline environment enables e-WASH to effectively emulsify oils and fats, providing cleansing power comparable to synthetic surfactants, while being gentle on the skin.

Additionally, e-WASH has demonstrated the ability to inactivate pathogens such as norovirus, making it suitable for various applications, including disinfection, cleaning, and deodorization.

The efficacy of disinfectants can be influenced by several factors, including the presence of organic matter, contact time, and the specific microorganisms targeted. Proper cleaning prior to disinfection is essential, as organic materials like dirt or bodily fluids can shield microorganisms from the disinfectant's action. Ensuring that surfaces remain wet with the disinfectant for the recommended contact time is also crucial for effective microbial inactivation.
